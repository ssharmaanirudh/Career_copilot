Business Intelligence Analyst — Regional Operations

About the role

Our regional operations team is growing and we're looking for a Business
Intelligence Analyst to help leadership make faster, better-informed
decisions. This is a highly visible role that partners directly with
regional directors and works across finance, operations, and customer
success to surface insights from our growing data footprint. Candidates
must have 5+ years of hands-on experience building dashboards in Tableau
to be considered for this role — our entire reporting stack is built on
Tableau and there is no ramp-up time built into this position.

Responsibilities

- Partner with regional directors to define KPIs and reporting priorities.
- Build and maintain dashboards used in weekly leadership reviews.
- Investigate anomalies in regional performance data and present findings.
- Coordinate with finance and operations teams to validate reported numbers.
- Document reporting processes and train new team members on dashboard use.
