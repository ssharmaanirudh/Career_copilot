Senior Data Platform Engineer

We're hiring a Senior Data Platform Engineer to own the infrastructure and
pipelines that power our analytics and ML platform end to end.

Essential Requirements:
- 5+ years of production Python development, specifically building and
  maintaining large-scale data pipelines (ETL/ELT) that run reliably at
  scale. This is the backbone skill for the role — nearly everything we
  build runs through Python pipelines you would own.
- Hands-on Kubernetes experience operating containerized data
  infrastructure in production, including deployments, scaling, and
  on-call ownership. Our entire platform runs on Kubernetes, and this
  role is the primary owner of that infrastructure day to day.
- Experience with a modern data warehouse (Snowflake, BigQuery, or
  Redshift).
- Strong SQL for pipeline debugging and data validation.

Nice to have:
- Experience with Terraform or another infrastructure-as-code tool.
- Familiarity with streaming systems (Kafka, Kinesis).

You'll work closely with the ML and analytics teams to keep the
platform reliable, observable, and fast to iterate on.
